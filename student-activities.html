Student Activities

Extracurriculars and religious activities that round out student life beyond the classroom.

Extracurricular Activities

Futsal

Weekly training and inter-school friendly matches.

Scouts

Pramuka activities building teamwork and outdoor skills.

Arts

Visual arts and performance club, with an annual exhibition.

Tahfiz Club

Extra memorization sessions for students aiming for advanced juz.

Science Club

Experiments, science fair prep, and robotics basics.

Religious Activities

Daily and weekly practices that build Islamic character alongside academics.

  • Tahfidz — daily guided Quran memorization.
  • Tamyiz & Tahsin — Quran reading and pronunciation classes.
  • Sholat Wajib & Sholat Sunnah — congregational prayers held at school.
  • Mabit — periodic overnight spiritual retreats.
  • Sirah Nabawiyyah — study of the life of the Prophet.
