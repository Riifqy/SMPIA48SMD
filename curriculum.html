Curriculum

A dual-track curriculum designed to meet Indonesia's national standards while stretching students with Cambridge-level material.

How Our Curriculum Works

Every student follows Indonesia's Kurikulum Merdeka as the foundation, enriched with Cambridge Lower Secondary content in Math, Science, and English, and framed by daily Islamic studies.

  • National curriculum (Kurikulum Merdeka) covers core competencies required by the Ministry of Education.
  • Cambridge-aligned Math, Science, and English classes add an internationally benchmarked layer.
  • Islamic studies (Aqidah, Fiqh, Quran & Tahfiz) are integrated daily, not treated as an add-on.
  • Project-based assessments run alongside standard exams each semester.

Curriculum by Grade

Grade 7Foundational literacy & numeracy, introduction to Cambridge Science and Math, Quran memorization Juz 30.
Grade 8Expanded science lab work, English for academic purposes, Tahsin and Tahfiz continuation.
Grade 9Exam preparation (national & Cambridge checkpoint), capstone project, high-school placement guidance.
