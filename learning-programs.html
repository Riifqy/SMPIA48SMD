Our Enrichment Programs

Alongside the core curriculum, students can join focused programs that build a specific skill or passion over multiple years.

Tahfiz Program

Structured daily Quran memorization with certified tahfiz teachers, targeting several juz by graduation.

STEM Enrichment

Extra lab time, robotics basics, and science-fair coaching for students drawn to Math and Science.

Language Immersion

Conversational English and Arabic clubs that meet weekly outside regular class hours.

Leadership Track

Student council and peer-mentoring roles designed to build public speaking and organizational skills.
